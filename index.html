A tracker watches you. A coach guides you.

Adding weight or distance too quickly can lead to burnout, sore joints and fatigue. Rucktoise takes out the guesswork. While you sleep, your Apple Watch records your night. When you wake, Rucktoise compares it with your own baseline and suggests a plan for the day: how much to carry, how long to walk, or whether to rest. It adapts to your life, so you can build steadily.

Continuous guidance. Zero guesswork.

  1. Overnight

    Your night, measured

    While you sleep, your Apple Watch records your resting heart rate, heart rate variability and sleep. Each morning, Rucktoise compares them with your own baseline.

  2. Morning plan

    Today's ruck, suggested

    You wake up to a simple Green, Yellow or Red status and a plan for the day: the weight, the time, the terrain, and a heart-rate ceiling to stay under.

  3. During your ruck

    A tap when it matters

    Your Watch shows your heart rate against today's ceiling. Go over it, and a tap on the wrist reminds you to ease off.

  4. After your ruck

    Your plan adapts

    You rate your effort and note any pain. Report pain, and your plan holds or steps the weight down automatically.

Regain. Retain. Build.

Fitness in your 40s and 50s isn't about obstacle courses or boot camps. It's about a steady foundation for everyday life.

Regain your resilience

Ease back into carrying weight. Time builds first, then weight, in small steps, as a lower-impact alternative to running.

Build foundational strength

Progress at a steady pace. More weight is offered only after two steady weeks, and only when you say yes.

Retain your energy

A heart-rate ceiling on every ruck, plus easy recovery days such as a light bike ride or an unloaded walk, helps keep you from overdoing it.

The Tortoise Ethos

We believe in coaching, not clinics. Rucktoise is designed as a supportive guide to help you discover the benefits of load carriage.

1

No Streak Shaming

Life happens. Planned rest days count as progress, and pausing your routine never results in a penalty.

2

Listen to Your Body

You report your effort and any discomfort after every session. If you need to step down the weight, Rucktoise automatically adjusts your plan.

3

Conservative by Default

When in doubt, the app suggests the easier path. The goal is to keep you moving comfortably and consistently for years to come.
