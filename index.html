Fitness that fits your life, on your schedule.

Balancing a demanding career, running a household, and raising a growing family leaves little time for a two-hour gym commute. You want to rebuild your foundational strength and endurance, but high-impact workouts often lead to burnout or joint strain.

Rucking, simply walking with a weighted pack or vest, is one of the most accessible, time-efficient ways to build cardiovascular health and physical resilience. But without a plan, it is easy to start too heavy and progress too fast. Rucktoise removes the guesswork, giving you a daily, personalized plan based on how your body is recovering.

Your Daily Path to Steady Strength

Readiness-Driven Daily Plans

Every morning, Rucktoise reads your overnight Apple Watch data, including sleep duration, resting heart rate, and HRV. It analyzes your recovery and suggests a specific load, duration, and pace for your ruck.

Steady, Gradual Progression

We prioritize steady adaptation. The engine gradually increases your load and distance over time, ensuring your body has time to adapt. You are always in control, and the app never suggests more than its rules allow.

Fits Your Existing Routine

Rucking leads, but your other activities support it. On non-ruck days, Rucktoise suggests simple aerobic base or recovery sessions using activities you already enjoy, like a low-impact indoor cycling ride or an unloaded walk around the neighborhood.

Ruck like a tortoise

The Tortoise Ethos

We believe in coaching, not clinics. Rucktoise is designed as a supportive guide to help you discover the benefits of load carriage.

1

No Streak Shaming

Life happens. Planned rest days count as progress, and pausing your routine never results in a penalty.

2

Listen to Your Body

You report your effort and any discomfort after every session. If you need to step down the weight, Rucktoise automatically adjusts your plan.

3

Conservative by Default

When in doubt, the app suggests the easier path. The goal is to keep you moving comfortably and consistently for years to come.
