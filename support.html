Inside the app, open Settings → How Rucktoise Works for answers about your plan, progress and health data, and Settings → Rucking Guide for how to ruck well.

Your data

Rucktoise has no account, so we can't see or reset your data from here. You can export or delete everything in Settings in the app. See the privacy policy for details.

Subscriptions

Apple handles payment, cancellation and refunds. To manage or cancel, open your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
